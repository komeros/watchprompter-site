GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Support
Can I use it without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Use the camera and teleprompter together on iPhone. Apple Watch adds wrist-based reading and control options.

How does the script follow my speech?

Voice tracking and word recognition advance the script based on your speech. You can also use adjustable automatic scrolling.

Can I control video recording from Apple Watch?

Yes. View your iPhone camera preview on Apple Watch and start or stop video recording.

Do I need an account?

No. WatchPrompter does not require an account or sign-in.
